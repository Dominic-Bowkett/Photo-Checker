EPC Photo Organiser

Right‑click any image and choose Add to EPC photo evidence, drag images into a tag, use Scan on an energytrust.assessapp.com page, or Import to pull images out of a PDF or Word doc. Each assessment is detected from the page heading and saved separately.

Floorplan

No floorplan filed yet. Right‑click the floorplan image and choose Add to EPC photo evidence → Floorplan.
